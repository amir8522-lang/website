Volunteer for Ward 7

This campaign is built on people. Conversations, outreach, and community engagement are what make a local campaign successful.

Whether you have a few hours a week or just want to help occasionally, there is a place for you on this campaign.

Ways you can help

  • Door-to-door canvassing in Ward 7
  • Phone calls and outreach
  • Community events and organizing
  • Social media and communications
  • Neighbourhood support and logistics

Every conversation matters. Your involvement helps bring real community voices into City Hall.
