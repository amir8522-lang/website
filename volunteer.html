Help Shape the Future of Ward 7

This campaign is built by people who live here—not outside consultants or political insiders. If you care about the direction of our community, there’s a place for you on this team.

Elections at the municipal level are decided by conversations: at the door, on the street, and between neighbours. A few hours of your time can genuinely make the difference.

What volunteering looks like

You don’t need political experience. Just a willingness to show up.

  • Door knocking: Talking to residents across the ward
  • Phone calls: Reaching supporters and undecided voters
  • Events: Helping organize and run local meetups
  • Community outreach: Connecting with neighbours and local groups
  • Logistics: Lawn signs, setup, and behind-the-scenes support

Why this matters

Municipal decisions affect your daily life—housing, transit, infrastructure, and how our neighbourhoods grow. This is one of the few places where a small group of committed people can have a real, immediate impact.

If you’ve ever felt like local politics happens without you, this is your chance to change that.
