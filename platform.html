A city built around the car is a city built for the few. We will push for a transit network that is frequent, free at point of use, and prioritized over private vehicle traffic.

  • Advocate for fare-free transit funded through a municipal vehicle levy
  • Dedicated bus lanes on all major arterials
  • Protected cycling infrastructure connecting all wards
  • Real-time arrival displays and accessible stop infrastructure
Transit Climate Infrastructure

The city is one of the largest employers in our community. We will use that power deliberately — to set standards, not race to the bottom.

  • Require a living wage for all city contractors and subcontractors
  • Preference for unionized firms in all municipal procurement
  • Support local worker co-operatives through city business licensing reform
  • Oppose use of municipal land for speculative commercial development
Labour Economy Co-ops

Climate change is not an abstract future threat — it is a present crisis that falls hardest on low-income communities and communities of colour. Environmental policy must be inseparable from social justice.

  • Enforce and strengthen municipal anti-idling and air quality bylaws in industrial zones
  • Green retrofits for social housing units — funded, not downloaded onto tenants
  • Community land trusts for urban agriculture and green space in underserved wards
  • No new fossil fuel infrastructure on city-owned land

  • Participatory budgeting for a meaningful share of the ward's capital budget
  • Lobbyist registry and strict disclosure rules for developer meetings
  • Council meetings in the evening and in accessible community spaces
