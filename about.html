About Kamrul Ahsan

Kamrul Ahsan, a graduate from University of Waterloo, is a community leader, engineer, and business owner with over 20 years of experience in engineering, manufacturing, international trade, and civic engagement.

He believes that local government should be practical, accessible, and responsive to residents. His campaign focuses on everyday issues: affordability, safer neighbourhoods, reliable services, and accountability at City Hall.

Kamrul has worked across industries and communities, bringing a results-oriented approach to problem solving and leadership.
